Delete your Loadbearer account

Last updated: 27 October 2026 · Version 1.0

This page is for people who use the Loadbearer workout log (loadbearer.app, and its Android app) and want their account and data deleted. You do not need to be signed in, or to have the app installed, to read it or to ask. There are two ways: do it yourself in the app, or ask by email.

1. Delete it yourself in the app

This is the fastest way. Deletion happens at once, with no waiting for a reply.

  1. Open Loadbearer and sign in.
  2. Open the Settings tab and scroll to the last group, Erase & delete.
  3. If you want a copy of your data first, tap Export all data first.
  4. Under Delete my account, type DELETE and tap Delete my account permanently.

If you only want to empty the account and keep the sign-in, use Erase everything in my account in the same place (type ERASE).

2. Ask by email, without signing in

If you cannot sign in, or you have uninstalled the app, email us from the address your account uses (the Google address, or the address you type to get an emailed code). Say “Delete my Loadbearer account”.

hello@loadbearer.app

We may reply to check that the request comes from you. We answer within one month, and tell you when the deletion is done. There is no charge. Deleting an account is permanent: we do not just deactivate it.

3. What is deleted

  • Your account: name, email address, profile picture address, and Google’s identifier and sign-in tokens for your account.
  • Everything you logged: workouts, check-offs, body weight and goals, programs, exercises you added, stretches, supplement and water logs, and your settings, including the older versions kept for recovery.
  • Your sign-in sessions, any unused emailed sign-in code, and your push-alert address and keys, if you turned alerts on.

These are removed from the live service immediately when the deletion is carried out.

4. What is kept, and for how long

DataKept
Nightly backups of the serviceUp to 30 days, then deleted automatically. Your data is therefore gone from backups within 30 days. If a backup were ever restored, deletions already requested would be applied again first.
Platform logs and error reportsAbout 30 days. Error reports are scrubbed of workout data and email, and are not linked to your account.
Delivery record of an emailed sign-in code (Resend, our email provider)Up to 30 days
Your emails to us, including a deletion requestAs long as needed to deal with them, and no longer than 24 months afterwards, unless the law requires longer.

Nothing else about you is kept. We do not keep a copy for advertising or analytics, because we do not use either.
