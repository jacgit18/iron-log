Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026 · Version 1.0

Iron Log is a workout log. This policy explains what personal data it collects when you use the signed-in version of the app, why, where it is kept, who can see it, how long it is kept, and the choices and rights you have. It is written to be read. If anything here is unclear, ask: the contact details are at the end.

1. Who is responsible

Iron Log is run by Joshua Carpentier, an individual (the “operator”, “we”). The operator is the controller of your personal data for the purposes of the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (“GDPR”) and the “business” for the purposes of the California Consumer Privacy Act (“CCPA”) and similar US state laws, to the extent they apply. Contact: joshuaxcarpentier@gmail.com.

2. The short version

  • We collect what is needed to sign you in and to store and sync the workouts you log. Nothing else.
  • There is no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. We do not sell your data and do not share it for advertising.
  • Your data is stored with two service providers (Google Cloud and Neon), in the United States.
  • You can export everything and erase it, or delete your account, yourself, at any time, in Settings. Deleted data is removed from the live service immediately and from backups within 30 days.
  • The app is for people aged 16 and over.

3. What we collect

Provided by you

The workout data you enter: logged sessions (exercise, date, weight, sets, reps, time, notes), check-offs on the weekly board, body weight entries, body-weight goals, programs you edit or save, exercises you add, stretches and supplement or water logs, and your settings (for example program mode, rest times and one-rep maxes).

From Google, when you sign in with Google

Your Google account’s email address, name, profile picture address and Google’s identifier for your account, and the sign-in tokens Google issues for the app (stored encrypted; we do not use them to access anything in your Google account). We do not receive your Google password. We request only basic profile access (identity, email and profile) and do not read your Gmail, contacts, calendar or anything else.

Collected automatically

  • Session records: while you are signed in, a session record with its expiry, the IP address and browser or device description it was created from. This is used to keep you signed in and to protect the service.
  • Server logs: the technical logs of the hosting platform (request times, addresses and errors), kept by the platform for about 30 days, used to run and secure the service.
  • Error reports: when the app on your device hits an unexpected error, it may send a short report (the kind of error, a message with all numbers removed, the page, the app version and technical stack lines). Reports are scrubbed so they do not contain your workout data or email, and are not linked to your account.

On your device

The app keeps a working copy of your data in your browser’s storage so it works offline and does not lose changes. This copy stays on your device; it is not a tracking tool and is not shared with any other site.

4. Health-related data

Body weight and training records can reveal information about your health or fitness. Some laws treat this as sensitive (“special category” data under the GDPR, “sensitive personal information” in California, “consumer health data” in Washington State and similar laws). We use it only to provide the app to you: to show your history, progress and targets and to sync it across your devices. We do not use it for advertising, profiling, insurance, employment or any decision about you, we do not sell it, and we do not share it with third parties other than the service providers listed below, which process it only on our instructions. You choose whether to enter it, and you can delete it at any time. Iron Log is not a medical product and gives no medical advice.

9. Your choices and rights

You can do most of this yourself, in the app, under Settings:

  • Access and portability: “Export all data” downloads everything you entered as a file (JSON, and also CSV or Excel), in a commonly used format.
  • Correction: edit or delete any entry in the app.
  • Erasure: “Delete my data” removes everything from the server and your device, or deletes your account entirely.

Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to restrict or object to processing, to withdraw consent at any time (without affecting what was done before), to be told what we hold about you, and to complain to a data protection authority (in the EU or UK, the authority in your country; in the UK, the Information Commissioner’s Office). In the United States, residents of California, Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Washington and other states may have rights to know, access, correct, delete and port their data, and to opt out of sale, sharing and targeted advertising. We do not sell or share personal data for advertising, so there is nothing to opt out of, and we will not discriminate against you for using these rights.

To use a right the app does not cover, write to the contact address. We may need to confirm it is you (for example by replying from the Google account email). We will answer within one month (GDPR) and within 45 days for US state laws, and tell you if we need longer. There is no charge unless a request is clearly excessive.

10. Security

All traffic is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Data is encrypted at rest by our providers. Each account can reach only its own rows, enforced by the database itself (row-level security) as well as by the application, and the application connects with a restricted database role. Sign-in and other sensitive requests are rate-limited, session cookies are marked secure and HttpOnly, and Google’s tokens are stored encrypted. Backups are kept in a private, access-controlled bucket. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your data in a way that creates risk to you, we will tell you and, where required, the relevant authority, without undue delay (and within 72 hours for the authority under GDPR).

11. Cookies and similar technologies

Iron Log sets a secure session cookie that keeps you signed in, and a short-lived technical cookie while you complete Google sign-in. Both are strictly necessary for the signed-in service, so no consent banner is needed. It is not used for tracking or advertising. There are no analytics, advertising or social-media cookies or scripts. Your browser’s local storage holds the working copy described in section 3, and a service worker caches the app’s files so it works offline.

12. Children

Iron Log is not intended for anyone under 16 and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has an account, tell us and we will delete it.

13. Automated decisions

We make no decisions about you by automated means that have legal or similarly significant effects. Targets shown in the app (for example suggested weights) are simple calculations from your own entries.

14. Changes

We will update this policy when the service changes. The date and version are at the top. For a material change (for example a new provider, a new purpose, or the introduction of payments) we will tell you in the app before it takes effect where we can, and ask for your consent again where the law requires it.
